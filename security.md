# Security Policy Template
## Reporting a Vulnerability
Thank you for helping us keep wigo4it/renovate-config secure. If you find a security vulnerability, please follow the steps below:
1. **Do not open a public issue**. Instead, please send an email to **security@wigo4it.nl** with the details of the vulnerability.
2. Include as much information as possible to help us understand and reproduce the issue. This may include:
   - A description of the vulnerability
   - Steps to reproduce the vulnerability
   - Any relevant screenshots or logs
   - Your contact information
## Response
We will acknowledge your report within 10 business days and provide an estimated timeline for addressing the vulnerability. We may ask for additional information or clarification during our investigation.
## Disclosure
We are committed to addressing security vulnerabilities in a responsible and timely manner. Once the vulnerability is resolved, we will notify you and provide a summary of the actions taken.
Thank you for your efforts to help us maintain the security of this project.
